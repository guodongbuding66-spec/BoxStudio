# BoxStudio original carton pictograms

The 21 SVG files in this directory and src/handlingArtworkV82.js are original
artwork by BoxStudio contributors, dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 Universal. You may copy, edit, redistribute and use them commercially,
including on packaging.

Full dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en

These are general-purpose carton instructions, not certified reproductions of
registered ISO artwork. Choose instructions appropriate to the actual package.
Earlier imported pictograms retain their source and license records in
assets/handling-v73/LICENSE.md and assets/handling-v73/sources.json.
